# Bouncy Ball

Motivator: Challenge and mastery. Author: noahschwed.

## Mechanic

Bouncy Ball is a simplified Flappy Bird–style game. Tap or click the board, press the Space key, press the up arrow key, or press Flap to stay airborne. Passing a pipe pair earns one point. Touching a pipe, ceiling, or ground ends the attempt; surviving 90 seconds completes it. Three independent toggles change difficulty: **Faster pipes** increases speed, **Narrower openings** reduces clearance, and **More frequent pipes** reduces the time between arrivals. Settings stay fixed during an attempt. Change them between attempts, then use Retry. Pause freezes play; Reset returns to setup and preserves selections.

## Dynamic

One playtester tried two configurations: all three toggles OFF and all three ON. With everything OFF, he appeared to lose interest before dying. With everything ON, he appeared more engaged, although he still seemed somewhat bored overall. He intentionally hit a pipe before reaching 90 seconds. Changing all three together prevents attributing the difference to an individual toggle.

## Aesthetic

He said the all-ON version was more fun and the all-OFF version was too easy. Greater challenge appeared to improve his enjoyment, but did not fully sustain his interest either.

## Sources

The course note states, “Goals whose outcome is uncertain, through variable difficulty, multiple levels of goals, hidden information, or randomness” (Malone 1980, pp. 66 to 67). Bouncy Ball explores this through selectable difficulty. Flappy Bird inspired the gameplay; graphics are original primitive shapes. Codex helped implement and test the game. I rejected Codex's suggestion to combine all three effects into one toggle, keeping them independent.

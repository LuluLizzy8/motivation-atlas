# Gone Fishing: when do the bites come?

Motivator: Chance. Author: LuluLizzy.

## Mechanic

You have 24 casts of bait. Press **Cast**, wait a moment for the line, and see whether a fish bites. Keep casting as long as you like, or press **Pack up** to stop early. The row of marks shows every cast so far.

The toggle, **Bites come at random**, changes when fish bite. On, each cast has a 1 in 4 chance. Off, every 4th cast bites, like clockwork. Both catch 6 fish per 24 casts on average. Set it, then press **New round**.

## Sources

From the Chance section of the course note:

- Variable-ratio reinforcement, the slot machine's schedule: "The repeat play of gamblers is strong evidence of the power of variable reinforcement" (Salen and Zimmerman 2003, ch. 24, "Rewards and Schedules").

The toggle swaps that variable-ratio schedule for a fixed one with the same average, so any change in how long a player keeps casting comes from the schedule alone.

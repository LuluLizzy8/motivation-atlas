# Fog Relics

Motivator: Discovery. Author: Hanxuan1003.

## Mechanic

Find three relics (◇) and return home (⌂) within forty moves. Move with the direction buttons, arrow keys, or WASD while focused inside the game. Rocks block movement without spending a move. Exploration fog reveals your tile and its adjacent tiles; discovered tiles remain visible. Turn fog off to reveal everything without changing movement, relics, or the move allowance. Restart repeats a map; Try other map opens its mirrored counterpart.

## Dynamic

After an explanation of the controls, one player tried fog on, then off. With fog on, they explored nearby unknown tiles, approached relics when discovered, encountered a rock and the blocked message, then collected all relics and returned home. With fog off, they reported that relics and obstacles were obvious and the goal easy to reach. The reported rounds lasted roughly one to two minutes. Prior exposure may have influenced the second round.

## Aesthetic

The player described the game as simple but said fog made them want to discover what lay hidden. Without fog, they reported losing that urge to explore and finding the easy, visible objective uninteresting. This account supports the intended curiosity contrast for this player; it does not establish a general effect.

## Sources

The course note's Discovery mechanics include “Secrets to find and areas to explore completely” (Koster 2013, p. 76). Fog Relics applies this through hidden relics and persistent exploration. Codex assisted implementation and tests. The implementation keeps its suggestion to isolate visibility while holding other rules constant.

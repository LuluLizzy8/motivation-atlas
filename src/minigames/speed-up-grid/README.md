# Speed-up grid: escalation

Motivator: Sensation. Author: joshuahkung.

## Mechanic

One of nine cells lights up. Tap it (or press its number, 1 to 9) before it jumps somewhere else. A hit scores; a wrong tap or a missed target costs one of three lives. The round ends after 45 seconds or three misses. The toggle is **Escalation**: on, the target stays lit 5% less time after every hit; off, it stays at the same speed all round.

## Dynamic

One playtester played a round with escalation on, then one with it off. With escalation on, they scored 7 hits: each hit made the next target harder to catch, so success itself brought the round to a quick end. With escalation off, they scored 59 hits at a steady pace. The toggle changed the outcome by a factor of eight.

## Aesthetic

The playtester said both rounds felt fast, because they were trying to click as fast as they could either way. So turning escalation off did not make the game feel calm; the difference was intensity. With escalation on, they said it sped up so fast it felt "crazy," and their reaction was "wtf." Escalation turned a quick game into a frantic one.

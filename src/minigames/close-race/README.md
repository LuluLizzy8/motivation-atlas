# Road Race: jump and squat

Motivator: Competition. Author: yvnvii.

## Mechanic

Choose a pace and start. Your runner passes twelve randomly placed obstacles. Jump bottom hurdles (**Space/Up**); squat under top inverted triangles (**Down**). Each press starts one fixed action window; holding a key will not extend or repeat it. The wrong action slows you for 1.8 seconds. The bot clears obstacles automatically. If it trails by three spaces, the checkbox briefly boosts its speed. Races last about 60–100 seconds. For a fair comparison, replay at the same pace with the boost changed.

## Dynamic

Players hit obstacles at first, then learned to time jumps and squats. With catch-up on, they were more careful as the bot closed the gap, since a collision could let it take the lead. With it off, they experimented, sometimes jumping or squatting at random.

## Aesthetic

They felt frustrated when an obstacle slowed them. With catch-up on, they couldn’t relax while ahead because the bot could gain ground, so they focused on avoiding mistakes. With it off, they felt more relaxed after building a lead.

## Sources

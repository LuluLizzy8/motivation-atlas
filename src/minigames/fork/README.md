# Fork

Motivator: Discovery. Author: saadsatter.

## Mechanic

You are lost in the woods. Choose left or right at ten forks to find home before a creature eats you. Tap a path, or use Tab and Enter. Tracks, a creature, or rustling start the chase. Dangerous routes remove six seconds and accelerate pursuit; safer routes add five seconds and slow it. The clock keeps running between decisions. Reach zero and you lose; choose the safe route at the final fork to reach home.

The **Mysterious Fog** checkbox hides path outcomes when on and reveals destinations and danger when off. Chase rules stay the same. Use **Replay this forest** to compare the same layout, or **New forest** to shuffle the paths.

## Dynamic

One other player found replaying the same forest helpful because they could map out good and bad choices. With fog off, however, the revealed hints reduced decisions to whether a path gave more or less time. They stopped reading the path descriptions and simply chose the time bonus to move forward. Revealing outcomes removed much of the risk of losing and the need to discover safe routes.

## Aesthetic

The player described the game as fun but said the timer starts a little too short. They appreciated learning the forest through replay. With fog off, they found it a little boring: the time hints made the choices obvious. Their feedback suggests that revealing outcomes weakens the uncertainty that makes finding a safe route satisfying.

## Sources

# Pocket Warrior

## Mechanic

Play eight pool layouts in a 60-second round. Choose Easy (1 base point) or
Hard (3); only that ball scores, scratches score zero, and gray obstacles
never score. Pull the cue stick back and release, or adjust the power slider
and press Shoot. Pull direction sets the angle; arrow keys aim, and Shift
makes smaller adjustments. Before a round, turn Highlighted pocket on or
off. On, a seeded shuffle highlights one of six pockets per layout (default
seed 42; enter another seed before starting). Sinking the selected ball
there triples its base value: 1 becomes 3, 3 becomes 9. Any other pocket
scores normally. Off, every pocket scores normally.

## Dynamic

With the toggle on, one player used more strategy and sometimes felt
motivated to switch target balls to chase the highlighted pocket. With it
off, the player said they played it like the original game.

## Aesthetic

With it on, the player said it “feels like a brand new game.” With it off,
they said it felt like playing the original game. These are one player's
reports, not proof that everyone will feel the same.

## Sources

From Challenge and mastery: “Goals whose outcome is uncertain, through
variable difficulty, multiple levels of goals, hidden information, or
randomness” (Malone 1980, pp. 66 to 67). The seed randomizes the bonus pocket;
aiming remains a player skill. Codex helped build Pocket Warrior; I kept its
seeded-shuffle suggestion so pockets rotate before repeating. Pool physics,
layouts, and pull-back controls adapt Beat the Clock by lzy2318831. No
external art or audio was used.

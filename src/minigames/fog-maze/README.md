# Fog maze: a small expedition

Motivator: Discovery. Author: ScarlettLeon7.

## Mechanic

Find the Moon pool, Crystal garden, and Echo chamber, then reach **E**, the exit.
You are **●**; uncollected landmarks are **◆**. Move one square with the North,
West, South, and East buttons, or focus a game button and use the arrow keys.
Walls block movement. There is no timer or penalty for retracing your steps.

With **Fog of war** on, you see your square and its four neighbors. Discovered
tiles remain visible. Turn it off to see the whole maze, including landmarks
and the exit. The toggle changes visibility immediately without changing your
position or progress. **Replay this maze** resets your expedition and explored
tiles, keeping the fog setting. Compare both settings on the same layout;
remembering the maze will also affect your second trip.

## Dynamic

With fog, we expect players to investigate branches and gradually build a map.
With the map exposed, we expect more route planning toward visible landmarks.
These are design predictions to compare with your play, rather than measured
outcomes. The nearby-directions text provides a text alternative for navigation.

In the author's playtest, fog prompted a choice between paths and more cautious
movement. With fog off, the author knew where to go, moved without hesitation,
and reported completing the game in fewer steps. This is one player's account;
familiarity with the same maze may also have helped after revealing the map.

## Aesthetic

The intended feeling is curiosity about what lies around a corner, followed by
the satisfaction of finding a landmark. What did you actually feel? Did changing
visibility change where you went or how the trip felt?

The author reported feeling more curious with the map hidden. Their cautious
movement suggests how that curiosity accompanied decisions about where to go.

# Find the Fish: Scent Hints

Motivator: Discovery. Author: zhstella.

## Mechanic

Find a dried fish hidden in a 4×4 grid in as few guesses as possible over three rounds. **Scent Hints** defaults ON: a miss one step away is strong, two or three steps is faint, and farther gives no scent. Revealed tiles retain icons. OFF reports only an empty tile. Changing the toggle restarts the game.

## Dynamic

Two anonymous players completed both modes. Player 1 needed 13 guesses ON versus 32 OFF; Player 2 needed 14 ON versus 23 OFF. Both began ON near the center, searched around strong scent, and relocated after wind. OFF, Player 1 clicked randomly; Player 2 tried edges, believing the fish was more likely there, then clicked aimlessly. Their two- and fifteen-guess OFF rounds showed luck replacing deduction.

## Aesthetic

Both preferred ON, calling the game fun, cute, and suited to its theme. Clues created curiosity, direction, anticipation, and happiness. OFF felt more random, uncertain, and comparatively boring; a two-guess find was exciting, while reaching the last two tiles felt comically unlucky. The orange 🐟 clue was first mistaken for a win, and 💨 also needed explanation—an unexpected usability result.

## Sources

The course note says hidden information revealed bit by bit “seems to provoke curiosity” (Malone 1980, p. 67), and that well-timed hints “renew their hope and their curiosity” (Schell 2019, p. 263). This game tests both claims by changing only hints.

Codex helped implement the code and suggested keeping an icon on each revealed miss. I kept it so past clues remain comparable without color. Emoji are system glyphs; no external materials were used.

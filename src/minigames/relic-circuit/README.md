# Relic Circuit

Motivator: Discovery. Author: nc3244.

## Mechanic

Deliver three relics (*) to camp (C) within 36 successful moves. Carry only
one at a time; return to camp to unload before collecting another.
You are @; # marks walls. Use direction buttons or focus a button and press
arrow keys. Blocked moves cost nothing. Restart repeats the map.

With **Fog of war** checked, your tile and its immediate neighbors are
revealed; explored tiles stay visible. Question marks conceal unexplored
tiles. Unchecking reveals the whole map. Movement, relic locations, and the
move budget stay the same.

## Dynamic

One other person played with fog on, then off. With fog on, they explored dead ends and searched away from their first relic, assuming two
relics would not be near one another. They delivered all three in 28 moves
with fog on. With fog off, they planned a route to deliver all three easily.
Playing the same map in both settings may also have helped route planning
in the second round.

## Aesthetic

With fog on, they reported feeling a little helpless because there was no clear strategy. Without fog, the task
felt easier because they could plan a route. Hidden information produced
helplessness rather than the intended curiosity.
